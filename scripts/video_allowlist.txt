# Curated Prelinger Archives films for the developmental video curriculum
# (2026-11-02). Everyday life with clear motion: animals, nature, farms,
# transport, towns and children at play. Not listed: weapons, hunting, meat
# processing, psychiatry, crime, sex education and advertising (incl. sponsor
# reels such as Garden Wise, A Ride for Cinderella, The Golden Years). War,
# propaganda, disasters and accidents may be added later at levels 1-2 below.
# Held out for the video probe
# (see scripts/real_sensory.py HELDOUT_FILMS): PrivateL1947, TexasFar1952,
# ParkCons1938.
#
# One film per line: `identifier level  # comment`. The level is the film's
# maturity (RealSensoryBank.MATURITY_LEVELS in scripts/real_sensory.py):
#   0  everyday life: animals, nature, farms, transport, towns, play  (stage 0+)
#   1  real-world complexity: work and industry, illness, natural hazards (stage 4+)
#   2  difficult history: war, propaganda, disasters (locked until stages 6-7
#      have framing episodes)
# A film without a level is treated as 2. Explicit sexual content is never
# listed at any level. The trainer reads levels from this file at startup,
# so retagging a film needs no cache rebuild; adding a film does.
